About

My first career was as a legal secretary. I went back for a computer science degree, built web applications for small businesses, and joined IBM in 2015 as a front-end engineer and the accessibility focal point for my product.

Since then I have moved toward the work around the work. I led IBM's front-end developer community, managed engineers, ran the company's design review program, served as chief of staff to the Chief AI Officer, and most recently led design for IBM's core software, through May 2026.

That path shapes how I lead. I understand engineering from the inside, I know how the business runs, and I treat the health of a team as a deliverable. Early on, I rebuilt the developer track of IBM Design's new-hire bootcamp around a real project for a real internal client. Within a year, participants' rating of how ready they felt rose from −43 to +33 NPS, and their managers' rating rose from −75 to +50. I also helped define IBM's Developer Profession career model, which gives developers a certified path toward Senior Technical Staff Member and Distinguished Engineer.
